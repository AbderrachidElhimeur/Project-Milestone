Moroccan Chicken Tagine

A comforting Moroccan dish made with tender chicken, fragrant spices, green olives, and preserved lemon. Serve it with fresh bread to enjoy the delicious sauce.

Prep time: 15 minutes   |   Cook time: 50–60 minutes   |   Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs or 4 chicken pieces
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 cup green olives
  • ½ preserved lemon, rinsed and cut into pieces
  • 1 cup water or chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken: In a bowl, mix the ginger, cumin, paprika, turmeric, black pepper, and salt. Rub the spices over the chicken.
  2. Cook the onion: Heat the olive oil in a tagine or heavy pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook for about 5 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Brown the chicken: Add the chicken and turn the pieces so they are coated with the onion and spices. Cook for a few minutes on each side.
  4. Simmer: Pour in the water or chicken broth. Cover and cook gently for about 35–45 minutes, turning the chicken once, until it is fully cooked and tender. Add a little water if the sauce becomes too dry.
  5. Add olives and lemon: Stir in the green olives and preserved lemon. Simmer uncovered for another 10 minutes so the sauce becomes flavorful.
  6. Finish and serve: Sprinkle with fresh cilantro and parsley. Serve hot with Moroccan bread, couscous, or rice.

Moroccan Cooking Tip

Preserved lemon has a strong, salty flavor, so rinse it before using and add extra salt only after tasting the sauce. If you do not have preserved lemon, use a little fresh lemon zest and juice as a substitute.
